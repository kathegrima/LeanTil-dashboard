📊 Understanding Your Dashboard
Overall Score

Your wellness score from 0 to 100. It shows how well you're maintaining good posture throughout your session.

Unlock badges as you hit milestones: First Session, Perfect Minute, Consistency Champion, and Posture SuperStar!
