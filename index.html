Understanding Your Dashboard
Overall Score

0–100 wellness score based on how consistently you maintain balanced posture this session.

KPI Cards

Four at-a-glance numbers: minutes in balanced posture today · active session time · your score vs yesterday · current streak of good posture days. Times over 60 minutes are shown in hours; over 24 hours in days.

Break Timer

Counts down 30 active sitting minutes, then suggests a stretch routine. Click "Take a Break" at any time to start the exercise routine.

Posture History

Click any day dot to see that day's full stats: score, quality breakdown, and a mini session timeline.

Seat Pressure Map

Visual representation of where your weight sits on the chair. Highlighted zones = more pressure. Balanced posture lights up all four zones evenly.

Connection Status

🟢 Connected — server OK, sensor data fresh.
🟡 Device offline — server reachable but sensor silent >15s.
🔴 Server offline — cannot reach LeanTil server, auto-retrying.
